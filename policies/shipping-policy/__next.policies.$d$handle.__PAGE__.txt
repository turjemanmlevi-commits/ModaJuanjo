3:T836,<h2>SHIPPING — MODESSAE</h2>
<p>We ship to addresses across Australia and New Zealand.</p>
<h3>1. Delivery times</h3>
<p>Orders are packed within <strong>1–2 business days</strong>. Once your parcel is on its way you'll get an email with tracking.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<strong>Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Adelaide, Perth and surrounds:</strong> 7–12 business days</li>
<li>
<strong>Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch and surrounds:</strong> 8–14 business days</li>
<li>
<strong>Regional and remote areas (Australia and New Zealand):</strong> 10–16 business days</li>
</ul>
<p>These are estimates, not guarantees. Customs, weather and courier delays are outside our control, and peak periods add a few days.</p>
<h3>2. Shipping costs</h3>
<p>Shipping is calculated at checkout before you pay, so there are no surprises. Any promotional free-shipping threshold is shown on the website at the time.</p>
<h3>3. Where we ship from</h3>
<p>Our items are dispatched from our supplier's warehouse. This keeps our prices where they are, and it's why delivery takes longer than a local warehouse would. We'd rather tell you that upfront than have you wonder where your parcel is.</p>
<h3>4. Duties and GST</h3>
<p>Any GST payable is included in the price shown at checkout — 10% in Australia, 15% in New Zealand. You won't be asked for extra on delivery.</p>
<h3>5. Tracking</h3>
<p>Your tracking link can take <strong>3–5 business days</strong> to start updating after you receive it. That's normal and doesn't mean anything is wrong.</p>
<h3>6. Wrong address</h3>
<p>Please check your address carefully at checkout. If a parcel is returned to us because the address was wrong or nobody could receive it, we'll contact you to arrange redelivery. Redelivery postage is at your cost.</p>
<h3>7. Lost or damaged in transit</h3>
<p>If your parcel doesn't arrive, or arrives damaged, email us at <strong>info@modessae.com</strong> with your order number. We'll sort it out — this sits under the consumer guarantees, so it's on us.</p>
